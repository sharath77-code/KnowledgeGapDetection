About EduGap AI

An AI-powered Intelligent Tutoring System designed to help students improve their learning by identifying weak concepts after online assessments and providing personalized study resources.

Platform Mission

Why Choose EduGap AI?

Traditional online tests only provide numerical scores without explaining why a student struggled or which concepts need revision.

EduGap AI goes beyond marks by analyzing student performance, identifying weak learning areas, and recommending personalized study materials that help students improve continuously. Students receive meaningful feedback instead of just marks.

Importance of Gap Detection

When a student fails to master a foundational concept early on, subsequent advanced topics become significantly harder to understand. By pinpointing exact knowledge gaps immediately after an assessment, EduGap AI prevents cumulative learning loss and builds a rock-solid academic foundation.

How Personalized Learning Helps

Every student learns at a different pace. Instead of generic textbook revisions, personalized learning targets the specific weak skills of each individual student with structured video lessons, concise master study summaries, and focused practice exercises.
