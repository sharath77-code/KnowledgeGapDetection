Privacy Policy

At EduGap AI, we are committed to protecting student data privacy and maintaining transparent information practices across our Intelligent Tutoring Platform.

1. Information Collection

We collect basic registration details (Student Name, Register Number, Department) and assessment responses solely to generate performance analytics and personalized study recommendations.

2. How We Use Data

Student data is used exclusively to evaluate conceptual accuracy, identify knowledge gap levels, and personalize study material recommendations. We do not sell or share student data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security

All session records and performance statistics are handled securely using encryption standards to ensure student confidentiality.

4. Contact Privacy Team

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact privacy@edugap.ai.
